Sponza Palace

A full architectural model, streamed into your browser. Scroll down — or swipe on your phone — to walk the camera all the way around it.

From 190 MB to 6 MB

The source is a 23 MB Wavefront OBJ with 262,267 faces and 98 MB of TGA textures. It was converted to glTF, welded, simplified, quantized, meshopt-compressed and re-encoded with WebP textures.

Touch friendly

Vertical swipes scroll the page and drive the orbit. Horizontal drags spin the building directly, on mouse or touch.
